ImproTron
Version 2.2 Release August, 2026
by Guy Winterbotham

ImproTron is Light and Sound software for managing multiple screens, media, and audio playback, designed primarily for ComedySportz (R) and other Improv Shows. It provides a comprehensive suite of tools to enhance live improvisational performances.

https://github.com/guywinterbotham/ImproTron
